NOMsters Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

The short version

NOMsters has no accounts and no sign in. To show food near you, it sends your location to our server, which asks Yelp. We do not keep your location. Your saved spots and settings stay on your phone. If you send us a "timing wrong" report, we keep what you sent so we can fix the hours. No ads, no tracking, no selling.

Who we are

NOMsters is made by Zain Adtani and Khizr. Questions or requests: za.backup1@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Places you save with the heart (My Spots)
  • Your settings, like "Open now only" and which maps app to use for directions

This is saved only on your phone. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your phone

Your location, when you allow it. NOMsters sends your location to our server so it can find food near you. Our server passes it to Yelp, which returns the list of places. We use it only to answer that search. We do not save it.

What you search for. Words you type in Search and the food buttons you tap go to our server and to Yelp in the same way, only to answer the search.

"Timing wrong?" reports, only if you send one. If you tell us a place's hours are wrong, we save:

  • the place (name, address, its Yelp link, and the hours the app showed)
  • the hours you typed and whether you checked with the owner
  • a photo, only if you choose to attach one

Zain and Khizr look at these to fix hours. Please do not put people's faces or personal details in the photo. The report does not include your name, your location, or anything that identifies you. We keep reports while we need them to fix hours. Email us if you want one deleted.

A scrambled version of your internet address, to stop abuse. To stop anyone from flooding the app with searches, our server counts requests per internet address. We never save the address itself. We save only a scrambled code made from it that cannot be turned back into the address. They are only used for about a day, and old ones are cleaned out automatically.

Who else is involved

  • Yelp provides the restaurant information. It receives your location and search words for each search. Yelp's own privacy policy applies to what it receives.
  • Supabase hosts our server and database, where reports and the scrambled counts are stored. Like any server company, it may keep short-term technical logs.
  • Expo delivers small app updates. When NOMsters checks for an update, Expo receives your internet address and a random install ID the app creates. It is not your name or your Apple ID. Expo's own privacy policy applies.
  • Apple runs the App Store and TestFlight and may collect crash reports or feedback you choose to send, under Apple's privacy policy.

If you tap Directions, a phone number, a recipe, or a web search, NOMsters opens your maps app, phone, or web browser. Those apps and websites have their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no email, no phone number
  • No ads and no ad tracking
  • No analytics or third-party tracking tools
  • We do not sell or share your information for advertising

Children

NOMsters is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • You can say no to location. NOMsters cannot show food near you without it, but nothing else is affected.
  • You can turn location off later in your iPhone's Settings.
  • You can ask us to delete a report you sent by emailing za.backup1@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change what NOMsters collects, we will update this page and the date at the top first.
